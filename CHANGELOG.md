# ParadoxLabs_StripeHyvaCheckout Changelog

## 1.1.0 - Jul 17, 2026

Customer-account payment options (paymentinfo) support on Hyvä themes, building on the shared ParadoxLabs_TokenBaseHyvaCheckout module: card list, delete, and add/edit via a setup-scope Payment Element (ConfirmationToken flow with inline 3DS). The "My Payment Options" nav-link removal is dropped now that the page works on Hyvä.

## 1.0.0 - Jul 17, 2026

Initial release: Stripe Payment Element (ConfirmationToken flow) support for Hyvä Checkout, with stored cards, wallets, inline 3D Secure, and strict CSP/Alpine CSP compliance.
